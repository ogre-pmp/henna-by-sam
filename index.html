About the Artist

Art made with intention & love

Hello, I'm Sam — a passionate henna artist based in Aïn Merane, Chlef, Algeria, dedicated to transforming skin into living art. With over eight years of experience, I blend traditional mehndi techniques with a contemporary aesthetic to create designs that are timeless, personal and breathtaking.

Every client's story is unique, and each design I create is a reflection of that story. Whether it's a bridal mehndi steeped in tradition or a delicate festival design, I bring the same devotion, care and artistry to every single session.

My henna is made from the finest natural ingredients — no chemicals, no shortcuts. Just pure craft, applied with love.

— Sam
